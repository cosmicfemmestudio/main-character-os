Questions

What is Main Character OS?

A manifestation planner and life OS that runs in your browser. It guides each manifestation through five steps (want it, own it, see it, act as if, and collect evidence) and uses your own birth chart and numerology as a guide for each area of life.

How does it use my birth chart?

Your chart is calculated on your device from your birth date, time and place. Each life area is read through its house in your chart: career through your Midheaven, love through your 7th house, money through your 2nd house, and so on, with an act-as-if suggestion for each.

What numerology does it include?

Your Life Path, Expression, Soul Urge, Personality and Birthday numbers, using the Pythagorean method with master numbers 11, 22 and 33, plus your personal year, month and day.

Can I make a vision board?

Yes. Add your own photos, words and colors to a main board and a board for each manifestation, drag to arrange, and save it as a phone wallpaper in the full version.

What else is inside?

Your current era and an era timeline, eight main-character archetypes, habits with streaks, money goals, a wins and evidence log, a daily act-as-if prompt, and letters to your future self that unlock on a date you choose.

Is my information private?

Yes. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Your birth details, photos and plans stay on your own device, and you can download a backup file at any time.

Does it work offline?

The full version installs on your Home Screen like an app and works with no internet connection.

What is the difference between the demo and the full version?

The free demo lets you try one manifestation through every step, a vision board with three photos, two habits, your Big Three and your Life Path. The full version has no limits and adds your chart for every life area, your full numerology profile, wallpaper export and future-self letters.
